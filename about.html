About Bruliki

Bruliki is a watch and accessories store that combines timeless style, quality and attention to detail.

Our Story

Bruliki was created with a simple idea: accessories should be more than practical objects. They should reflect personal style and become part of everyday life. Bruliki was created by Muslimova Aliya, Adenova Albina and Kerimkulova Sabina to convey this idea through exceptional luxury accessories collections.

Our Values

Quality

We carefully select our products to offer reliable watches and accessories made with attention to detail.

Style

We choose watches and accessories that combine modern design with timeless style.

Attention to details

We believe that small details make a difference, from the design of each accessory to the experience of our customers.

Quality Guarantee

At Bruliki, we care about the quality of every product we offer. Our watches and accessories are carefully selected to meet our standards of design, materials and reliability.
